How to Get Tears Beneath the Mask

Complete the Call of the Lost Commission

You can get the Tears Beneath the Mask arc by finishing the Call of the Lost commission in New Herland District, which is done by defeating the Nestbound Bird. This fight requires you to actively dodge projectiles, but Hotori will give you alot of time when the boss reaches 50-75% HP!

Call of the Lost Commission Guide

Purchased From the Arc Selection


Tears Beneath the Mask can be purchased from the Mall's Arc Shop - Arc Selection using Tri-Keys.
