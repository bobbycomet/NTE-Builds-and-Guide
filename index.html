Beginner's Guide — Welcome to Hethereau

New to Neverness to Everness? This section covers everything you need to hit the ground running before touching any build theory.

01
Reroll for Your Core Team

The game gives up to 40 free pulls after the prologue. Use them to reroll for at least one S-rank DPS. Your starting roster shapes your early experience: a good DPS paired with a healer and a buffer clears everything comfortably.

02
Know the Three Roles

DPS (Damage): Your primary damage dealers. Invest in these first. S-rank DPS like Nanally, Chiz, Jiuyuan, and Baicang are top-tier picks.
Survival: Fadia and Edgar keep the team alive. You need one.
Buffer: Sakiri, Hotori, and Adler amplify everyone else.

03
Focus One Team, Not Six

This is the most important early tip. Build one team of 4 characters to a high level before branching out. Spreading resources thin is the most common beginner mistake. Pick one strong DPS, one healer, one support, and one flex unit.

04
Level Up All Character Skills

All characters have multiple skill types you must level up individually: their basic attacks, ultimate attack, Critical Riposte, support skills, and passive skills that help the team. Leveling attributes alone is not enough. Each skill category scales your power independently, so invest in all of them as resources allow. Passives in particular can carry the whole team.

05
Get an Apartment, and the Boxer Anomaly

Get an apartment as soon as possible and unlock the Boxer Anomaly inside it. The Boxer Anomaly raises your Crit DMG directly, and when you earn medals, you level it up for even more. There is a cap in the first apartment, but it is one of the best early sources of guaranteed Crit DMG in the game, no RNG required.

06
Fish Early, Grind Fons Smart

Fish often. Around 80 fish early on can net you roughly 30,000 to 80,000 Fons. Do not rush to get the cafe first: it does not generate much income early. Once you have around 1 million Fons, which is doable in a day, get a good car and grab food at the vending machine so you can keep grinding. The grind itself also generates Fons quickly, so treat it as part of your daily loop.

07
Story First, Side Content Second

Clear the main Episodes first. They unlock core systems and reward Annulith (pull currency), Beetle Coins, and Hunter EXP. Spinoffs (side quests) are secondary but still reward good resources. City Tycoon is fun but optional early on.

08
Understand the Combat Loop

Basic attacks fill your Cycle Rate. When full, switching characters triggers their Esper Cycle (enhanced state). Hitting enemies depletes their Break Meter: fully breaking an enemy stuns them and boosts damage taken. Always dodge at the right moment to trigger a Critical Riposte counter.

09
Gather Hunter's Guides and Beetle Coins

Hunter's Guides level up your Espers; Beetle Coins pay for leveling. Both come from Episodes, Spinoffs, Anomaly Dungeons, and Pilgrimages. Never let these sit uncollected, as they expire from daily sources if ignored.

10
Hunter Level = Content Unlock

Your Hunter Level determines which Episodes and game modes you can access. Raise it consistently by completing quests. Your Appraisal Level increases enemy strength but also drops rewards, so push it when your team can handle the enemies.

11
City Tycoon Boosts Combat Too

The City Tycoon mode (apartment, jobs, businesses) provides passive combat bonuses via apartment upgrades. These buffs are real and meaningful: unlock your apartment early and invest in it steadily. They compensate for gaps in your Module stats. Hathor will be hard to pull, but if you get her, you will get the Hamster Anomaly free, and you will get better odds at getting better modules. Just hunt down haunted vending machines to level the hamster up. There is a cap similarly to the Boxer Anomaly.

12
Visit the Witch Daily

The Witch's House exchanges Oracle Stones for Annulith, EXP, and Fons. She also gives a daily Lost Tale quest, a party Bless, or a Treasure Hunt. Do this every single day: the compounding value is huge over weeks.

13
Arcs Before Cartridges

Prioritize getting a good Arc (weapon) on your main DPS first. It provides a larger base stat boost than individual Module slots. Then collect Cartridge sets to activate 2-piece and 4-piece bonuses. Modules come last since they are RNG-dependent.

14
Find Anomaly zones. These are on the map with a reverse horseshoe shape curtain. These are where character mats, module mats, and cartridges can be found. Farm these after you hit max level in your current appraiser level.
15
Once you do have cafe(s), be sure that you restock for 72H. This will continue to make money while you are logged out. This still does not make as much as fishing if you want a faster Fons pool.

What is the Gacha System (Scarborough Fair)?

NTE uses a gacha banner system called Scarborough Fair. S-rank Espers (characters) and Arcs are the top-tier pulls. The pity system guarantees an S-rank within a set number of pulls. The game offers a 50/50 mechanic: if you lose the 50/50, your next S-rank is guaranteed to be the featured character. Save your Annulith (pull currency) for characters you plan to build, not impulsively.

F2P Tip: You get a free S-rank Esper selector at launch. Use this wisely: check the tier list and pick a character whose playstyle you enjoy AND who synergizes with your other units.

Understanding Elements and Reactions

Every character deals a specific elemental damage type: Psyche, Cosmos, Chaos, Anima, Lakshana, or Incantation. Some Cartridge set bonuses are triggered by elemental reactions: matching your character's element to their Cartridge set and watching for reaction windows (Nova, Scorch, Remora, Stain) is how you unlock the full power of your gear.

Golden Rules of Building

01
Crit Rate & Crit DMG Are King
Target 70%+ Crit Rate and 140%+ Crit DMG before worrying about anything else on a DPS. These multiply everything else: a 10% Crit DMG gain on a high Crit Rate build outperforms a large flat ATK increase.
02
Type DMG then DMG% then ATK%
If a character's skills specify an element (e.g. Psyche DMG), prioritize that type's Cartridge set bonus. Then DMG%. Then ATK%. These are all multiplicative with each other when layered correctly.
03
Level Base Stats First
ATK%, Crit DMG, and DMG% all multiply your base ATK and HP. Level your character, Arc, and push Breakthrough before worrying about substat optimization. A poorly leveled character with good substats underperforms a leveled character with decent substats.
04
Cartridge Rank Determines Substat Ceiling
B-rank Cartridges give the lowest substat rolls. A-rank gives moderate. S-rank gives the highest possible substat values. The set effect is the same at all ranks, but the stats inside the Modules you slot in scale with Cartridge rank.
05
Apartment Fills the Gaps
Your apartment upgrades provide passive combat buffs that don't come from Modules at all. Don't compensate for bad Modules by wasting rolls on flat DEF or flat HP; let apartment perks and support characters cover survivability.
06
Break Intensity Is Role-Specific
Only invest in Break Intensity on characters whose skill descriptions explicitly reference Break DMG or Break reactions (primarily Daffodill). For all other DPS characters, treat Break Intensity rolls as wasted substat budget.
07
ATK% vs HP%: Role Determines Priority
DPS characters always want ATK%. Survival characters like Fadia want HP% because her mechanics scale with HP pool. Healers like Edgar benefit from HP% since heal output is partially HP-based. Never mix these priorities up.
08
Match Arc Type to Character Arc Slot
Each character uses a specific Arc type (Bose, Plasma, Liquid, Gas, Solid). You cannot equip any Arc on any character. Always check the Arc type before purchasing or pulling an Arc for someone.

Damage Formula (Simplified)

Final DMG = Base ATK × (1 + ATK%) × (1 + DMG%) × (1 + TypeDMG%) × CritMult × DEF_Factor

CritMult = 1 + (CritRate × CritDMG) Examples: 70% CR × 140% CD → +0.98 avg multiplier 100% CR × 200% CD → +2.0 avg multiplier ← endgame goal

Because Crit feeds into a separate multiplier layer that stacks with DMG% and ATK%, it has outsized value. Every % of Crit Rate you gain is worth more the higher your Crit DMG is, and vice versa, as they multiply each other.

Character Build Guides

Click any character to expand their full build guide. Each character has multiple build paths: tabs inside show S, A, and B-rank tiers so you always have something to work toward.

Cartridge Sets — All Ranks

Cartridges come in B, A, and S ranks. The set effect is identical at all ranks; S-rank Cartridges allow higher substat rolls on the Modules slotted inside them. Always work toward the S-rank version; use B and A as stepping stones.

Key Concept: A B-rank Cartridge with its 2-piece set bonus active is still better than no Cartridge. Equip the matching element Cartridge at whatever rank you have, then upgrade over time.

Arcs (Weapons) — All Ranks

Arcs are weapons. S-rank Arcs have the strongest passives and highest base stats. A-rank Arcs are strong alternatives that are far more accessible. B-rank Arcs are placeholder options for new players; replace them as soon as possible.

Modules — Understanding RNG Gear

Modules are slotted inside Cartridges. They provide random substats: this is NTE's equivalent of Genshin artifact farming. The quality of substats you can roll is capped by the Module's rank (B, A, or S), which in turn is determined by the Cartridge rank they came from.

B-Rank Module

Lowest substat values. 2-3 starting substats. Each upgrade adds small amounts. Use as filler while farming better gear. Good enough for story content.

A-Rank Module

Moderate substat values. 3-4 starting substats. Viable for mid-game and some endgame content. Upgrade these if you get good stat combinations (Crit Rate + Crit DMG + ATK%).

S-Rank Module

Highest possible substat values. 4 starting substats. Best for endgame. Farm these specifically: one perfect S-rank Module with Crit Rate + Crit DMG + ATK% + DMG% is worth keeping even if set bonus is off.

Module Types and Slot Shapes

Modules come in different shapes (Type II, III, IV) that fit specific slots in a Cartridge's grid. You do not choose which stats a Module has: they are randomly determined when obtained. The shape determines which slot it fits; the rank determines the stat ceiling.

Farming Efficiency Tips

Do not re-roll or discard every Module immediately. An A-rank Module with Crit Rate + Crit DMG + ATK% is worth keeping even over an S-rank with HP% + DEF% + flat ATK. The combination of substats matters more than the raw rank alone at mid-game. At endgame, you want S-rank modules with the right combination.

Best-in-Slot Target: S-rank Module in the correct Cartridge set with Crit Rate + Crit DMG + ATK% + Type DMG% as four substats. This is the dream roll: expect hundreds of farming runs to achieve it on multiple characters.

Deep Dive: Advanced Optimization

Crit Rate vs Crit DMG — How to Balance

The optimal Crit balance point depends on your current values. In general, if your Crit Rate is below 50%, prioritize Crit Rate over Crit DMG. Above 70%, prioritize Crit DMG. The marginal value of each changes as the other increases. A rough target for most DPS is 70% Crit Rate and 150%+ Crit DMG as a mid-game goal, with 100% CR and 200%+ CD as endgame.

Expected Crit Contribution = CritRate × CritDMG 70% CR × 150% CD = 1.05 (solid mid-game) 85% CR × 170% CD = 1.445 (strong) 100% CR × 200% CD = 2.0 (endgame cap)

Break Mechanics Deep Dive

When you deplete an enemy's Break Meter, they enter a Broken state: they cannot move or attack, and all damage they receive is amplified. Break Intensity increases how much you deplete the Break Meter per hit. Characters like Daffodill have skills that add Insight during break, applying additional Break DMG that scales with Break Intensity. For everyone else, breaks are a bonus side effect, not a stat to build around.

Breaker Team Tip: Pair Daffodill with a Chaos-element supporter for Scorch reactions to trigger the Diabolos Cartridge's full 24% RES reduction. The break window + RES shred combination is how Daffodill reaches her damage ceiling.

Support/Buffer ATK Scaling

Sakiri's Feast of Gluttony gives all allies an ATK boost calculated from her own base ATK. This means investing in Sakiri's ATK stat directly scales every teammate's damage. Her ATK% from Cartridge set stacks (Crimson: Twin Butterflies) and her Arc compound this effect. Build Sakiri for raw ATK% before anything else.

Hotori's World's Tide replays recorded ally skills: the replayed damage uses Hotori's own stats for the Cosmos portion. So Hotori benefits from both her own Cosmos DMG% investment AND the teammate's stats at the time of recording. She is effectively a dual-scaling support.

DoT vs Direct Damage — Lacrimosa Example

Lacrimosa's Nightmare DoT ticks do not always Crit the same way direct attacks do in all DoT implementations. Because of this, her priority shifts toward ATK% first (which scales the DoT tick value directly) before Crit. Her direct hits from Wordless attacks do benefit from Crit normally. This split-priority makes her unusual among DPS characters.

ETD-4 vs Eibon Faction Synergy

ETD-4 (Baicang, Fadia, Lacrimosa, Skia): Running multiple ETD-4 members together triggers passive cooperative attacks. Baicang's Moderate Working passive fires unique combo attacks when paired with Skia (stealth entry), Fadia (HP threshold), or Lacrimosa (bat form switch). This effectively adds free damage with no stamina cost and rewards faction-stacking.

Eibon (Hotori, Daffodill, Nanally, Sakiri, Adler, Edgar): Eibon composition benefits from Sakiri as a dedicated ATK buffer. She amplifies the entire Eibon roster and synergizes with Adler's shielding to create a self-sustaining offensive-support loop.

Recommendation: Build your first team around a full or partial faction (3+ members from one faction) to unlock passive synergy bonuses on top of your gear investment.

Charge Efficiency — When Does It Matter?

Charge Efficiency reduces the number of attacks needed to fill your Ultimate gauge. For characters whose ultimates are core to their damage (Hotori's World's Tide, Hathor's Emergency Delivery) or whose buffs are delivered through ultimates (Sakiri's Feast of Gluttony, Speedy Hedgehog's 4-piece team ATK), Charge Efficiency is a legitimate 4th or 5th priority substat on top of the standard Crit/ATK build. For pure DPS who use ultimates as part of a rotation but do not depend on them to buff the team, it is lower priority.
